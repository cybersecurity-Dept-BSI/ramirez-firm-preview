The Ramirez Firm serves Ontario residents from its Covina office. Whether the case involves an employment claim, a personal injury after a crash, or a criminal charge, John Ramirez handles the case personally.

Ontario — legal landscape.

Ontario sits in San Bernardino County. Employment claims filed in this jurisdiction go through the San Bernardino County Superior Court. Personal injury cases involving crashes on the 10, 60, and 15 freeways are common. Local employers and industries — logistics (Ontario Airport is a major hub), warehousing, retail, and food processing — generate the bulk of wage-and-hour and discrimination matters we see out of Ontario.

Common cases from Ontario.

  • Wrongful termination — including retaliation after complaints about pay, harassment, or safety
  • Sexual harassment at work in Ontario-area employers
  • Wage and overtime violations at hourly and shift-based workplaces
  • Car accidents on the 10, 60, and 15 freeways
  • Personal injury from premises liability and negligence
  • Criminal defense for Ontario-area DUI, felony, and misdemeanor charges
